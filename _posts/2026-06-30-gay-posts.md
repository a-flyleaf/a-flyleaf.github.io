## ok it's whenever
read: All of ↑that was drafted at the start of the month (the 2nd, to be exact). It is now the end of the month (June&nbsp;30). I don't remember exactly why I set this post aside without publishing it, beyond some vague and nebulous frustration with it leaving off without something of <span style="font-family:serif;">Substance™</span>. So then I went and did and made a bunch of stuff and now there are more things to share. Hooray!

### media consumption dispatches
- [Song rec](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sVNElajRjE), found through an animatic for...
- ...[that one viral circus show](https://www.glitchprod.com/digital-circus), which wrapped up this month. Its premise is *extremely* up my alley; I delayed watching it both because I don't like getting into things before they're finished, and because I had the vague sense it might take over my life. I'm writing this on the heel of the finale, and... y'know? The writing stumbles sometimes, I'm not #hashtagObsessed with it like I thought I'd be, but it was pretty good. Fun show. Watching an episode a day was a chill time, and it's given me food for thought re:how to approach my own writing. It did not hit the notes I expected it to hit, and I can see what it was trying to do instead. Would recommend if you want a character-driven comedy-drama drizzled in 90s/early-aughts technological chaos, served up in snappy indie animation with a smile.
	- Also (I can't believe I'm saying this), watch the [merch ads](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLHovnlOusNLiVNtW9iRdFImnKYHEgkdI1).
