- Read the entirety of <b><i>[How Dare You?!](https://bethanydellemanwriter.com/home/how-dare-you-%e6%88%90%e4%bd%95%e4%bd%93%e7%bb%9f-cheng-he-ti-tong/)</i></b>, a Chinese webnovella inexpliably hosted on a Jane&nbsp;Austen Fan&nbsp;Fiction website, on a whim. Surprisingly good! Nice mix of humor and drama, actually made me care about a central romance (historically a difficult feat), fun stuff. I enjoy the existential horror of the male protag's life immensely.
	- Also unsure if lines like "CharacterName: '?'" are a translation quirk, but I enjoy those too. I do. Works great for comedy.
